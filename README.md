The Sales Dashboard is a data-driven analytics project designed to provide clear insights into sales performance and business trends. It uses Python, SQL, MS Excel, and Power BI to collect, clean, analyze, and visualize sales data.

The dashboard highlights key metrics such as total sales, revenue trends, product performance, regional analysis, and customer behavior. Interactive visuals and filters help stakeholders quickly understand patterns, compare performance, and make informed decisions.

This project focuses on data accuracy, analytical storytelling, and real-world business problem solving, improving reporting efficiency and enabling up to 90% accurate insights for decision-making.
